# FraudLens Research Plan

## 1. Research Objective

To study existing approaches used in financial fraud detection and investigation, understand their capabilities and limitations, and identify suitable technologies for developing a practical AI-assisted financial fraud investigation system.

---

## 2. Research Questions

### RQ1

What approaches and technologies are currently used for financial fraud detection and investigation?

### RQ2

What capabilities do these approaches provide for transaction analysis, fraud detection, entity relationships, and evidence analysis?

### RQ3

What are the main limitations of existing approaches and systems?

### RQ4

Which technologies are suitable for developing a practical financial fraud investigation system?

---

## 3. Research Areas

### 3.1 Financial Fraud Detection

* Rule-based detection

* Behavioral analysis

### 3.2 Transaction & Entity Analysis

* Transaction patterns
* Temporal analysis

* Fund-flow analysis

### 3.3 Document & Evidence Analysis

### 3.4 AI-Assisted Investigation

* LLMs
* RAG

* AI-assisted investigation

---

## 5. Paper Selection

The research will focus on **2–3 core papers studied in depth**, supported by additional references where necessary.

A paper will be selected based on:

* Relevance to financial fraud/investigation
* Clear methodology
* Available dataset or experimental information
* Reported results
* Relevance to one or more FraudLens research questions

Papers that are unrelated, unreliable, duplicate, or lack sufficient research information will be excluded.

---

## 6. Research Extraction

For each core paper, the following information will be recorded:

| Field       | Information                  |
| ----------- | ---------------------------- |
| Paper       | Title, authors, year         |
| Problem     | Problem addressed            |
| Method      | Approach/model used          |
| Dataset     | Dataset and characteristics  |
| Results     | Main findings                |
| Limitations | Reported limitations         |
| Relevance   | Relevance to FraudLens       |
| Evidence    | Page/section/table reference |

The papers will be **studied and understood by the project team**, rather than simply generating content from abstracts or AI summaries.

---

## 7. Existing-System Study

Selected existing financial fraud detection/investigation systems will be studied separately to understand:

* Main purpose
* Fraud detection capabilities
* Transaction analysis
* Entity/relationship analysis
* Evidence/document handling
* Investigation support
* Limitations

Information about existing systems will preferably be taken from official documentation or authoritative sources.

---

## 8. Research Gap

The research gap will be identified **after analysing the selected papers and existing systems**.

The comparison will determine whether existing approaches adequately combine:

* Financial transaction analysis
* Entity/relationship analysis
* Investigation documents
* Evidence retrieval
* AI-assisted investigation

The final research gap will be based only on findings supported by the selected sources.

---

## 9. Expected Research Outcome

The research will provide:

* Understanding of existing financial fraud investigation approaches
* Comparison of their capabilities and limitations
* Identification of a practical research gap
* Selection of suitable technologies
* Requirements for the FraudLens system
* Foundation for the Sem 7 prototype and Sem 8 implementation

The research will focus on developing a **practical B.Tech-level prototype**, rather than attempting to build a production-grade financial investigation platform.
